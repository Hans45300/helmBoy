# On/Off Controls

## Overview

![On/Off control example](../pictures/On%20off.png)

Toggle buttons and switches for enabling/disabling features.

## Types

- **Power buttons**: Enable/disable entire sections
- **Option toggles**: Switch between operational modes
- **Feature switches**: Activate/deactivate specific features

## Visual States

- **On**: Illuminated or highlighted
- **Off**: Dimmed or grayed out
